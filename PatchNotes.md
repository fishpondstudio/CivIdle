**Circus Maximus**
- Old: +5 Happiness. All Musician's Guilds, Writer's Guilds and Painter's Guilds get +1 Production and Storage Multiplier
- New: +5X Happiness. All Musician's Guilds, Writer's Guilds, and Painter's Guilds gain +X Production and Storage Multipliers. X = this wonder's level
- Changed: Circus Maximus can now be upgraded, and its bonuses scale with its level.

**Colosseum**
- Old: Chariot Workshops are exempt from -1 happiness. Consumes 10 chariots to produce 10 happiness. Each unlocked age gives 2 extra happiness
- New: Chariot Workshops are exempt from the -1 Happiness penalty. Consumes 10X Chariots to produce +5X Happiness. Each unlocked age provides an additional +X Happiness. X = this wonder's level
- Changed: Colosseum can now be upgraded. Its Chariot consumption and Happiness bonuses scale with its level.

**Statue of Zeus**
- Old: Spawn random deposits, from among those that have been unlocked, on adjacent empty tiles. All Tier I buildings adjacent to Statue of Zeus get +5 Production and Storage Multiplier
- New: Spawns random unlocked deposits on adjacent empty tiles. All buildings within X tiles gain +5 Production and Storage Multipliers. X = this wonder's level
- Changed: Statue of Zeus can now be upgraded. Its range scales with its level, and the Tier I restriction has been removed.

**Parthenon**
- Old: Two great people of Classical Age are born and you get 4 choices for each. Musician's Guilds and Painter's Guilds get +1 Production, Worker Capacity and Storage Multiplier and are exempt from -1 Happiness
- New: Each level provides 2 Classical Age Great People, with 4 choices for each. Musician's Guilds and Painter's Guilds gain +1 Production, Worker Capacity, and Storage Multipliers and are exempt from the -1 Happiness penalty
- Changed: Parthenon can now be upgraded, granting its Great People reward at every level.

**Abu Simbel**
- Old: Double the effect of Ramesses II. All wonders Adjacent to Abu Simbel get +1 Happiness
- New: Each level provides +100% Ramesses II's (Great Person) effect. All wonders within X tiles gain +1 Happiness. X = this wonder's level
- Changed: Abu Simbel can now be upgraded. Its Ramesses II bonus and Happiness range scale with its level.

**Great Sphinx**
- Old: All Tier II or above buildings within 2 tiles get +N Production Multiplier. N = Number of its adjacent buildings of the same type
- New: All buildings within X tiles gain +N Production Multiplier. For each affected building, N is the number of adjacent buildings of the same type. X = this wonder's level
- Changed: Great Sphinx can now be upgraded. Its range scales with its level, and the Tier II restriction has been removed.

**Opet (Egyptian Festival)**
- Old: Opet: Great Sphinx provides +N Building Level Boost to all Tier II or above buildings within 2 tiles of the Sphinx. N = Number of its adjacent buildings of the same type
- New: Opet: Great Sphinx also grants each affected building a Building Level Boost equal to the Production Multiplier it provides
- Changed: Opet now follows Great Sphinx's level-scaled range and expanded set of affected buildings.

**Great Wall**
- Old: All buildings within 1 tile range get +N Production, Worker Capacity and Storage Multiplier. N = the number of the different ages between the current age and the age where the building is first unlocked. When constructed next to Forbidden City, the range increases to 2 tiles
- New: All buildings within X tiles gain +N Production, Worker Capacity, and Storage Multipliers. N is the difference between the current age and the age in which the building is unlocked. X = this wonder's level. Building it next to Forbidden City increases its range by 1 tile
- Changed: Great Wall can now be upgraded. Its base range scales with its level, while Forbidden City adds 1 tile to that range.

**Yellow Crane Tower**
- Old: +1 choice when choosing great people. All buildings within 1 tile range of the Tower get +1 Production, Worker Capacity and Storage Multiplier. When constructed next to Yangtze River, the range increases to 2 tiles
- New: Provides +1 choice when selecting Great People. All buildings within 2 tiles gain +X Production, Worker Capacity, and Storage Multipliers. X = this wonder's level. Building it next to Yangtze River increases its range by 2 tiles
- Changed: Yellow Crane Tower can now be upgraded. Its base range is now 2 tiles, Yangtze River adds 2 tiles, and its multipliers scale with its level.

**Porcelain Tower**
- Old: +5 Happiness. When constructed, all your currently earned extra great people at rebirth will become available for this run (they are rolled following the same rule as permanent great people)
- New: +5 Happiness, plus +1 Happiness for each additional level. When constructed, all Extra Great People earned at Rebirth become available for this run, following the same roll rules as permanent Great People
- Changed: Porcelain Tower can now be upgraded, and its Happiness bonus scales with its level. Its Great People effect is unchanged.

**Lunar New Year (Chinese Festival)**
- Old: Lunar New Year: Great Wall provides double the boost to buildings. Porcelain Tower provides +1 level to all great people from this run
- New: Lunar New Year: Great Wall and Yellow Crane Tower also grant affected buildings a Building Level Boost equal to the Production Multiplier they provide. Porcelain Tower grants +1 level to all Great People from this run
- Changed: Great Wall's doubled multipliers have been replaced with an equivalent Building Level Boost, and Yellow Crane Tower now also grants a Building Level Boost during Lunar New Year. Porcelain Tower's festival bonus is unchanged.
